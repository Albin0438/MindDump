Life itself is designed to be disturbing and complex. The pain never leaves until death arrives. I see death as a saviour. Like me, did you ever think about the inherent meaning of life?

  • What is the purpose of life?
  • What meaning does it have?
  • What is the end goal?

Then I am happy that I am not alone with such thoughts. This type of thought has a name in philosophy: nihilism.

What Is Nihilism?#

It is the philosophical belief that life has no inherent meaning or purpose. 1/1000 people feel this thought in their entire life. They start to feel like their life is cursed, rather than moving on with material and comfortable things offered by the world.

To explain it simply, imagine you buy a board game, but after opening the box, there is no rulebook, scoreboard, or finish line. This is the simple way to define what a life is.

The universe didn’t give us a script, manual or guide about life. We are just a tiny dot inside a spinning rock in a massive universe without an end. The one correction that will apply here is we didn’t ask for the life; it was given to us for free as a result of two individuals pleasure-seeking, then life forced us into disturbing moments without our permission.

Why Nihilism Matters.#

Nihilism is not just another passive, disturbing thought. But it’s an open door to the reality we experience today. While many people cover their real tragedies and disturbing thoughts with pleasures offered by the modern world, nihilism forces us to see the reality and ignore the covering created by authorities and billionaires.

When many individuals hear about such a philosophical thought, they often label the persons who believe it as lazy or irresponsible towards life. But it is not. Nihilism is much more than a state of boredom, meaninglessness, laziness, or hate. Let’s look at why.

It validates our exhaustion.#

We live in a disturbing and truly imperfect world. Nothing is right. Everything is moving forward in an unstable manner. Everywhere violence, hate, fake, consuming humans for money and power, manipulation, etc. On the other side society actively says to each other, “We have to be productive, build streaks, optimise our time, and leave a legacy.” This is really an exhausting amount of weight to carry.

This is where nihilism stands with more priority. Nihilism forces us to question all those requirements, needs, and things society pushes us into. It gives you permission to put that weight down. The more we dig, the more we start to understand that the real problem is not what we believed to be the problem, but it is the path we followed along with others, even without a second thought.

Breaks the perfect streak illusion.#

Most of the feelings we have each day have nothing to do with our life, existence, or even the universe. These overwhelming amounts of emotions like overthinking, trauma, fear, depression, etc., are just products of things that have no meaning, but still, we always hold onto them in our lives. Like job-related stress, childhood experiences that are already over, thinking about someone else, getting distracted by social media, etc.

For example, when I felt so overwhelmed by past disturbing thoughts or events, I skipped my blog writing habit for one day, and it ended up turning into a 3-day skip. This then caused me guilt about what I did. Why did I drop my useful habits just for some useless, already-over random thoughts? This happened because the illusion or mind created fake importance by overwriting my knowledge and understanding about nihilism, which is a result of actively participating in a sick society.

But on the other hand, a good knowledge of nihilism will neutralise the effects of such conditions in our life and mind.

Nihilism shifts the power back to us.#

This is the main and most important benefit of nihilism. It gives the power over our own life back to us, a power that we never really owned at all. It matters because once you realise society’s rules and expectations don’t matter at all, you get the freedom in your life to choose what to do, how to do it, etc. Because in the end, nothing matters at all. Death is the end of everything. But we never know how it will reach us, when it will arrive, how painful it will be, etc. So why bother at all? Just live as you want.

The Unspoken Reality.#

Forgetting death exists.#

The truth is that most of the population has actually failed to find meaning in life. No goal, no tasks, no rules, etc. But for the authority and conquerors, this was not acceptable at all. So they built a structure, a structure that made the entire society follow it without questions at all.

Born -> study -> work -> have children -> raise them to fit into societal expectations -> die.

This is the pattern everyone lives today. No questioning, no different opinions at all.

On the other side, many individuals are in a rush towards money, having more property, valuable materials, etc. They need respect and acceptance from the entire society. They need comfort and no disappointments. But life was never designed to be like that. Life is all about suffering and unexpected events. We don’t even know where death is hiding, waiting for a perfect time to hunt us.

So the one and only thing we can morally understand from this is that modern life is specifically curated to challenge death. They are truly forgetting the whole point of life. It’s like building a massive fortress on sand.

The Black Sheep.#

‘A black sheep’ is an idiom. It points to a member of a group or a family who is different, who doesn’t fit in, and who is often considered a troublemaker or an outsider. The phrase is perfect for someone who questions the entire existence and life.

Dear Nihilists, We are the black sheep of the real world. We often think deeply about such things existing in life. And we try to question or prove their importance. For 90% of the population, this is annoying, and it will hurt their years of beliefs and egos, so eventually, they will mark us as weird or mental. They isolate or ignore us. But still, we never hesitate to change, as it is not just a stupid belief like they have. It is the reality.

I am proud to say that I am a black sheep, btw.
